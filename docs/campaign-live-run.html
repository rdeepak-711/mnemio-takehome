The AI-written sequences

  • Source: npm run full-run on 25 Oct 2026 against a scratch copy of the database (10,000 seeded contacts, SEED=42, deliverability and hourly pacing on), with a live OpenRouter key on the free models. The page is a frozen export of that run; it does not connect to anything.
  • Emails are never sent for real: the "provider" is a mock that decides bounces and replies from a hash of the seed and each email's key, so a re-run gives the same outcomes. The AI's answers are real.
  • Days are simulated UTC days. The campaign starts on Mon 2 Nov 2026 at 09:00; the seed's history before that is not shown, but its contacts are in the funnel.
  • Colours follow role, not rank: the same blue means "first email" on every chart, and labels and values are always also written out.
